How rain moves through the Atmosphere

The Atmosphere is the open network Bluesky runs on. Spare AI usage can travel across it the way water does: up from whoever has more than they need, and down where a friend can use it.

  1. Evaporation

    Unused usage lifts off

    Your AI plan resets on a schedule. Whatever you haven’t used by then is gone, so that’s the part worth sharing.

  2. Condensation

    It gathers in your circle

    Post a forecast: what you run and how much you have to spare. It sits with the people you already follow.

  3. Precipitation

    It falls on a sidequest

    Pick something a friend would like done in their repo, and send your agent after it with a careful brief.

  4. Collection

    It runs back as work

    A pull request lands, your friend marks it cleared, and their project grows a little. Next reset, it goes around again.

What you do here

  1. Post a sidequestPoint at a Tangled or GitHub issue, say what done looks like, and which models you’d like used. Public for your mutuals, or private to a group.
  2. Forecast your rainSay what you run, how much you have to spare, and who you’d like to help first: people without big plans, students, projects that share your values.
  3. Send your agentOne click tells your friend you’re on it and copies a command that starts Claude Code, Codex or any MCP agent with a careful brief. Turn in the pull request when it’s up.

Every sidequest, take and profile is a record in its owner’s own account (dev.rainshare.*). Your circle comes from who you follow on Bluesky.
