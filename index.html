Your AI plan resets whether you use it or not. Rainshare shows you what the people you follow would like done in their repos, so the agent usage you have left over lands somewhere useful.

Or look around an example circle first. Rainshare works with Bluesky, Tangled and GitHub.

How rain moves through the Atmosphere

The Atmosphere is the open network Bluesky runs on. Spare AI usage can travel across it the way water does: up from whoever has more than they need, and down where a friend can use it.

  1. Usage you won’t get to lifts off. Your AI plan resets on a schedule, and whatever you haven’t used by then is gone, so that’s the part worth sharing.

  2. It gathers in your circle. Post a forecast of what you run and how much you have to spare, and it sits with the people you already follow.

  3. It falls on a sidequest. Pick something a friend would like done in their repo, and send your agent after it with a careful brief.

  4. It runs back as work. A pull request lands, your friend marks it cleared, and their project grows a little. Next reset, it goes around again.

What you do here

  • Post a sidequest

    Point at a Tangled or GitHub issue, say what done looks like, and which models you’d like used. Public for your mutuals, or private to a group.

  • Forecast your rain

    Say what you run, how much you have to spare, and who you’d like to help first: people without big plans, students, projects that share your values.

  • Send your agent

    One click tells your friend you’re on it and copies a command that starts Claude Code, Codex or any MCP agent with a careful brief. Turn in the pull request when it’s up.

Every sidequest, take and profile is a record in its owner’s own account (dev.rainshare.*). Your circle comes from who you follow on Bluesky.
